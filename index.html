Hitch Cap FAQ

What exactly is a hitch cap?
A hitch cap — also called a hitch plug or trailer hitch cover — is a plug that slides into the open square receiver tube on the back of your truck or SUV. It keeps out road grime, water, and bugs, and gives the back of your rig some personality instead of a blank black hole.
Will it fit my truck or SUV?
Our caps are made to fit any standard hitch receiver found on most trucks and SUVs. Full fitment details will land on each product page at launch.
Are they built to last outdoors?
That's the whole point. Every cap is designed to be weatherproof and rust-resistant — engineered to shrug off rain, mud, salt, and sun so it still looks sharp after a season of towing. We're finalizing materials now and will publish full specs at launch.
